Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2025

Aegis is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

Information We Collect

  • Account info: Name, email, subscription tier.
  • Financial data: Credit card balances and transactions via Plaid, with your explicit consent. Never sold or shared.
  • Vehicle info: VIN numbers fetched from the NHTSA public database.
  • Push tokens: Device notification preferences via OneSignal.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide bill alerts, reward notifications, and safety bulletins
  • To process subscriptions via RevenueCat and Stripe
  • To improve app performance

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share only with trusted providers (Plaid, OneSignal, RevenueCat, Stripe) strictly to operate the service.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. Financial data is handled via Plaid's bank-grade infrastructure.
