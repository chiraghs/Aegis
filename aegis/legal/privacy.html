Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2025

Overview

Aegis ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Aegis personal finance application.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, and subscription tier when you create an account.
  • Financial data: Credit card details, balances, and transaction history connected via Plaid with your explicit consent. This data is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Vehicle information: VIN numbers and vehicle details you enter, fetched from the NHTSA public database.
  • Insurance data: Policy details you manually enter into the Insurance Vault. Stored locally on your device.
  • Usage data: App interactions and session data used to improve the experience.
  • Push notification tokens: Device tokens and notification preferences managed via OneSignal.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalize the Aegis experience
  • To send bill due-date alerts, reward notifications, and NHTSA safety bulletins
  • To process subscription payments via RevenueCat and Stripe
  • To calculate your Unified Net Worth and Aegis Coin rewards
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted providers to operate the app. Each has their own privacy policy:

  • Plaid — Bank account connection and transaction data
  • OneSignal — Push notifications and in-app messaging
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management
  • Stripe — Payment processing
  • NHTSA — Vehicle recall and specification data (public database)

Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256. Financial data is handled exclusively through Plaid's bank-grade secure infrastructure. We never store your bank credentials.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@aegisapp.io

Children's Privacy

Aegis is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via push notification or email.

Contact Us

For privacy inquiries: privacy@aegisapp.io
